Metal Slug (Nazca Corporation, April 1996) is a side-scrolling run-and-gun renowned for
extraordinarily detailed hand-drawn pixel art on Neo Geo hardware — up to 380 simultaneous
sprites and a 3,840-color on-screen maximum from a 65,536-color total palette.

## In this game

Metal Slug is not an assigned Keystroke Kingdom cabinet, but its Neo Geo MVS hardware sits
at the technical peak of the 2D-sprite era alongside 16-bit-era games like
[[street-fighter-ii]], just before Act 4's shift to full 3D ([[early-3d-era]]).

## The facts

- Developer: Nazca Corporation (founded 1994 by ex-Irem staff; became an SNK subsidiary in
  1996, the year Metal Slug shipped). Released April 19, 1996 (Japan, MVS).
- Visual style: extraordinarily detailed hand-drawn pixel art, cartoonish and humorous, with
  fluid slapstick-comedy animation. Detailed parallax-scrolling backgrounds. 320x224
  resolution.
- Input: joystick + three buttons (shoot, jump, grenade).
- Technical: SNK Neo Geo MVS hardware — Motorola 68000 CPU @ 12 MHz + Z80 @ 4 MHz. Up to
  380 sprites on screen simultaneously, 96 per scanline; maximum sprite size 16x512 pixels.
  65,536-color total palette, **3,840 colors on-screen maximum** (256 palettes x 15
  colors). Yamaha YM2610 sound chip.
- **Correction (internal contradiction fixed):** an earlier draft's Color Palette field said
  "4,096 on-screen," contradicting its own Technical Characteristics field of 3,840; 3,840
  is the correct figure per Copetti's Neo Geo hardware analysis.
- Iconic: the SV-001 "Metal Slug" tank; getting captured turns the player character fat
  (with increased punching power). Music by Takushi Hiyamuta.
