Galaga (Namco, 1981) is the fixed shooter and successor to [[galaxian]], famous for its Boss
Galaga tractor-beam capture mechanic that lets players rescue their ship to form a
double-firepower dual fighter.

## In this game

Galaga is the **Level 4 cabinet**, teaching A and the eighth key (never called
"semicolon" in-game). Completing it unlocks the full home row and triggers the Mystery
Cabinet's glow — the biggest visual step yet in Act 1, with 8+ rainbow colors and starfield
depth. See `.claude/rules/levels.md` Level 4.

## The facts

- Developer: Namco. Released September 1981 (Japan), November 1981 (North America) — the
  successor to [[galaxian]]'s board.
- Visual style: colorful sprites against a black starfield, insectoid alien designs in
  formation, more graphically rich than Galaxian with smoother animation.
- Input: two-way joystick (left/right) plus fire button; can fire two shots on screen
  simultaneously (unlike Galaxian's single-shot limit).
- Technical: Namco Galaga arcade system board (successor to the Galaxian board). 16x16
  pixel sprites with limited on-screen sprite count; developers worked around limits by
  combining ship + bullet into a single 16x16 sprite. The same board powered Bosconian and
  [[dig-dug]].
- Iconic: the kill screen at Stage 255 (8-bit integer overflow); Challenging Stages (bonus
  rounds) with preset attack patterns and no enemy fire.
