Bubble Bobble (Taito, June 1986), designed by Fukio "MTJ" Mitsuji, is the two-player
cooperative platformer starring dragons Bub and Bob, notable for tracking whether one or two
players were present to determine which of its layered endings the player earned.

## In this game

Bubble Bobble is not an assigned Keystroke Kingdom cabinet but represents mid-80s Taito
design alongside [[frogger]] and the co-op design lineage that continues through
[[gauntlet]] and [[double-dragon]].

## The facts

- Developer: Taito — designed by Fukio "MTJ" Mitsuji. Released June 16, 1986 (Japan).
- Visual style: bright, cheerful, cartoon-like 2D graphics — green dragon (Bub) and blue
  dragon (Bob), bubbles as a primary visual element, 100 distinct hand-built level layouts
  (no procedural generation claimed by any source).
- Input: joystick + two buttons (jump and bubble); designed around two-player cooperative
  play.
- Iconic: trapping enemies in bubbles then popping them; the "true ending" requires two
  players and, for the full best ending, a hidden second 100-level "Super" mode run —
  designed to encourage social play.
- Technical: Taito arcade hardware (1986 era), sprite-based 2D graphics with independent
  enemy AI patterns per level.
