Dance Mat Typing is a free, web-based touch-typing tutor produced by the BBC, aimed at
children, that teaches the same home-row-first curriculum as the commercial canon tutors
under a UK-public-broadcaster education framing.

## In this game

Dance Mat Typing is one of the four canon tutors ([[mario-teaches-typing]],
[[mavis-beacon]], [[typingclub]], and this one) cited as corroboration that Keystroke
Kingdom's home-row-first, finger-pair-outward key progression (see
[[touch-typing-pedagogy]] and `.claude/rules/levels.md` "Why This Order") is the
uncontroversial standard across the genre — including its child-focused, freely-available
corner of the market, which is the closest sibling to Keystroke Kingdom's own target
audience.

## The facts

- Producer: BBC (BBC Bitesize education platform).
- Format: free, web-based, aimed at children learning to type.
- Curriculum: shares the home-row-first, finger-pair-outward sequence documented in
  [[touch-typing-pedagogy]].
- **Sourcing note:** the official `bbc.co.uk` URL was not directly fetchable by the
  research tooling used for this project; the claim is corroborated instead by two
  independent institutional sources (a K-12 resource center and a UK county council
  education page) that both identify Dance Mat Typing as a BBC product and link to the
  official page. Worth one manual click-check if precision matters later.
