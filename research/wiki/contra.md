Contra (Konami, February 1987) is the run-and-gun classic whose NES port carries the
[[konami-code]] (up-up-down-down-left-right-left-right-B-A), granting 30 lives — one of the
most famous cheat codes in video game history.

## In this game

Contra is not an assigned Keystroke Kingdom cabinet, but its NES-version Konami Code is
directly relevant to the game's easter-egg/hacker-culture research thread — see
[[konami-code]].

## The facts

- Developer: Konami. Released February 20, 1987 (Japan), March 1987 (North America).
- Visual style: detailed military/sci-fi action with multiple perspectives in one game —
  side-scrolling, pseudo-3D corridor view, vertically scrolling, and fixed-screen battles.
- Input: 8-way joystick + two buttons (shoot and jump); joystick also aims in 8 directions
  while shooting.
- Iconic: the Konami Code granting 30 lives (NES-specific, confirmed by NESdev forum
  community analysis); two-player simultaneous co-op; the somersault jump animation.
- Technical: arcade ran on Nintendo VS. System board. NES North American version used a
  cheaper UNROM board (half the ROM of the Japanese version); Japanese Famicom version used
  Konami's VRC2 memory controller (allowing animated backgrounds). NES explosion sprites
  ran at 30fps versus 60fps gameplay to work around sprite limits.
