Apple Computer was founded April 1, 1976 by Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak (with early
partner Ronald Wayne) in the Jobs family garage in Los Altos, California — beginning
with the hand-built Apple I, unveiled that July at the Homebrew Computer Club.

## The facts

- Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak founded Apple Computer, Inc. on April 1, 1976, joined
  briefly by Ronald Wayne, who held a 10% stake before selling it back for $800 just
  twelve days later.
- The Apple I was hand-assembled in the Jobs family garage in Los Altos, California,
  and unveiled in July 1976 at a Homebrew Computer Club meeting; about 50 boards were
  sold to the Byte Shop that year.
- Early investor Mike Markkula (a former Intel executive) provided crucial early
  funding and business guidance shortly after founding.
