The first computer mouse was designed by Douglas Engelbart in 1964 and physically built
by Bill English from redwood in the SRI machine shop, then shown publicly at "the Mother
of All Demos" on December 9, 1968.

## In this game

Level 11's history card spells `mouse` — M unlocks at Level 11. Card text: *"Douglas
Engelbart carved the first computer mouse from wood and showed it in 'the Mother of All
Demos.'"* See [[computing-history-moments]] for the full 17-card table.

## The facts

- **1964:** Engelbart conceives the design; Bill English physically builds the first
  wooden prototype at SRI.
- **December 9, 1968:** the mouse is publicly demonstrated at "the Mother of All Demos,"
  Brooks Hall, San Francisco.
- The card says Engelbart "showed it" at the 1968 demo (true), not that he built it in
  1968 — sources consistently date the build to 1964. The card doesn't name Bill
  English, a common simplification (CHM itself sometimes shorthands to "Engelbart's
  mouse").
- Verdict: accurate as literally written. No change needed; the 1964-vs-1968 nuance
  (design/build year vs. demo year) is noted for completeness.
