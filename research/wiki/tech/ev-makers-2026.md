A dated, deliberately perishable snapshot of who is actually shipping electric vehicles as of
**September 2026**, written because Keystroke Kingdom's Act 7 word bank may name real
brands and a kids' game should not teach a brand that evaporates. The headline: **Tesla and
Rivian ship today; Scout and Slate do not ship yet (2027 and late-2026 targets); Zoox is not
a car brand at all** ([[zoox-robotaxi]]); and the recent graveyard is real — Fisker (2024),
Canoo (2025), Nikola, Lordstown, and Ford's own F-150 Lightning, killed in December 2025.

## In this game

Brand words are proper nouns → capitals → **Shift, Level 24+** territory (or lowercase in
free play). See [[automotive-vocabulary]] for the vetted list, the trademark caution, and the
"prefer the concept over the logo" rule: `battery`, `motor`, `charge` and `regen` never go
out of business, while a brand name can be a dead reference inside two years. If a brand
word ships, prefer the ones with the longest odds of surviving a child's whole
learning-to-read arc.

## The facts (as of 2026-09-12)

**Shipping now**

- **Tesla** — founded 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning in San Carlos, California;
  Elon Musk invested $30M in 2004 and joined as board chairman. Still shipping at scale:
  ~838,000 vehicles delivered in the first half of 2026, over 95% of it Model 3 and Model Y;
  Cybertruck volumes are small and falling (3,519 US units in Q1 2026, the lowest since
  deliveries began in November 2023). Lost the global BEV sales crown to **BYD** for 2025.
- **Rivian** — builds the **R1T** pickup, **R1S** SUV and commercial vans at Normal,
  Illinois. Started production of the smaller, cheaper **R2** on **22 April 2026** (five days
  after an EF-1 tornado hit the plant), with 2026 guidance of **62,000–67,000 total
  deliveries**. R2 Performance Launch Edition from $57,990; the advertised ~$45,000 base R2
  is not due until late 2027.
- **BYD** (China) — the world's largest seller of all-electric vehicles as of 2025
  (~2.26M BEVs; ~4.6M including plug-in hybrids). Not a US consumer brand.
- Also shipping EVs in the US and worth knowing as ordinary words: Ford, Chevrolet, Hyundai,
  Kia, Nissan, Honda, Toyota, Volvo, Polestar, Lucid, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Volkswagen.
  *(Named from the general market; individual model claims were not separately verified in
  this pass — treat the list as orientation, not citation.)*

**Announced, not yet shipping**

- **Scout Motors** — a Volkswagen Group-backed revival of the International Harvester Scout
  name. Two vehicles: the **Traveler** SUV and **Terra** pickup, unveiled 24 October 2024,
  to be built in Blythewood, South Carolina (~$2B plant, ~4,000 jobs, 200,000 vehicles/year
  at peak). Scout's own site says **"Initial production targeted for 2027, subject to
  change"** and takes **reservations only** — you cannot buy one today. Both models are
  planned in pure-EV and EREV (range-extender) forms.
- **Slate Auto** — Bezos-backed startup; a deliberately bare-bones two-seat electric pickup
  (base version: ~150 miles of range, no power windows, no infotainment screen, no paint),
  production targeted for **late 2026** in a former printing plant in Warsaw, Indiana; over
  150,000 refundable $50 reservations by December 2025. Its "under $20,000" framing was
  removed after the $7,500 federal EV tax credit ended in July 2025; later coverage quotes
  ~$26,400 with destination.

**Gone, or pulled back — the volatility evidence**

- **Fisker** filed for Chapter 11 in **June 2024**.
- **Canoo** filed for Chapter 7 and ceased operations in **January 2025**.
- **Nikola**, **Lordstown Motors**, **Proterra**, **Arrival**, **Lion Electric** and
  **Electric Last Mile Solutions** all filed for some form of bankruptcy protection in the
  2022–2025 window.
- **Ford discontinued the all-electric F-150 Lightning** on **15 December 2025**, less than
  four years after launch, as part of a ~$19.5B charge against its EV programs; the
  replacement is an extended-range (gas-generator) EREV.

## Why this page is dated in its own title

Everything above is a market fact with a shelf life. Any downstream use in game text should
either (a) cite this page's `verified:` date, or (b) prefer concept words over brand words.
Re-verify before shipping brand names to a child.
