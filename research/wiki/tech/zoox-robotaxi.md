Zoox is **not a car brand**. It is an Amazon-owned robotaxi *service* whose purpose-built
electric vehicle has no steering wheel, no pedals, and no driver's seat — you cannot buy
one, you ride in one. Its own homepage leads with the line "It's not a car. It's a robotaxi
designed around you." In July 2026 NHTSA granted Zoox a temporary exemption from several
Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards, and on 10 August 2026 it began charging fares in Las
Vegas — reported as the first purpose-built vehicle without a steering wheel or brake pedal
to collect fares in the United States.

## The facts

- **What it is.** Amazon-owned Zoox operates a robotaxi ride service. The vehicle was
  engineered from the start with no steering wheel, no pedals, no conventional driver seat
  and no fixed front or rear — it is bidirectional, boxy, carries up to four passengers in
  two facing rows, and is fully electric.
- **Cities.** As of this pass, zoox.com lists Las Vegas, San Francisco, Austin, Miami,
  Atlanta and Los Angeles. Free public rides ran in Las Vegas and San Francisco before
  fares began.
- **Regulatory milestone.** NHTSA granted Zoox a temporary exemption from several Federal
  Motor Vehicle Safety Standards — reported as a two-year window (31 July 2026 – 31 July
  2028) permitting deployment of up to 2,500 of the vehicles per year.
- **Paid service.** Zoox began charging for rides in Las Vegas on **10 August 2026**,
  reported as the first time a purpose-built autonomous vehicle with no steering wheel or
  brake pedal collected fares anywhere in the US.
- **Caveat worth keeping.** Coverage of the exemption noted an accompanying safety-data gap
  — the exemption is a regulatory permission, not a safety verdict. Nothing in a kids' word
  bank should imply an endorsement of driverless safety.
