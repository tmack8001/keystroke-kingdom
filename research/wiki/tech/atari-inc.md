Atari, Inc. was founded in June 1972 by Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney in Sunnyvale,
California — the company that shipped Pong later that year and, four years later,
[[breakout|Breakout]], the very first cabinet Keystroke Kingdom's player types on.

## In this game

**Level 8 history card, word `atari`.** All five letters (a, t, a, r, i) are available
the moment T unlocks at Level 8 (see `.claude/rules/levels/act1.md` Level 8) — t is the
bottleneck. A deliberate narrative callback: the company whose founding this card tells
is the same company that made [[breakout]], the Level 1 cabinet the player has already
played by the time they reach Level 8.

## The facts

- Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney founded Atari, Inc. in June 1972 in Sunnyvale,
  California, to develop and market arcade games — Pong, released later that year,
  was their first commercial hit.
- Ted Dabney left the company in 1973; Nolan Bushnell sold Atari to Warner
  Communications and eventually departed in 1979 — meaning Bushnell was gone from
  Atari's ownership by the time [[breakout|Breakout]] shipped in 1976 under his prior
  leadership, and entirely by the time this game's Level 1 era begins.
- Atari's Breakout (1976), designed by Bushnell and Steve Bristow with an early
  prototype built by Steve Wozniak, is [[breakout|this game's Level 1 cabinet]].
