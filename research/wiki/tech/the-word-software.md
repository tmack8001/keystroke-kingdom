Statistician John W. Tukey is credited with the first published use of "software" in a
computing sense, in a January 1958 article in *The American Mathematical Monthly* —
naming the instructions inside a computer as something distinct from, and "at least as
important" as, its physical hardware.

## The facts

- John W. Tukey published the term "software" in "The Teaching of Concrete
  Mathematics," *The American Mathematical Monthly*, January 1958 — describing computer
  programs as distinct from, and "at least as important" as, the "hardware" of tubes,
  transistors, and wires.
- Tukey is also credited with coining "bit" (binary digit) in an earlier memo, making
  him responsible for two of computing's most fundamental everyday words.
- "Software" filled a real gap: before it, there was no common, concise word to
  distinguish a computer's instructions from its physical components.
