The floppy disk shipped commercially in 1971, developed at IBM under Alan Shugart with
David L. Noble as lead engineer and Warren L. Dalziel as lead drive inventor — the
removable storage medium a generation of computer users grew up with.

## In this game

Level 10's history card spells `floppy` — P unlocks at Level 10. Card text: *"Before
clouds, files lived on bendy plastic squares. One floppy held less than a single photo
does today."* See [[computing-history-moments]] for the full 17-card table.

## The facts

- **1971:** the floppy disk ships (development began 1967), holding 80KB, developed
  under Alan Shugart at IBM.
- Modern phone photos commonly run 2–10MB — a 25–125x difference from 80KB, easily
  supporting the "held less than a single photo does today" comparison.
- Verdict: accurate. 1971 is correctly the shipping/commercial-introduction year. No
  change needed.
