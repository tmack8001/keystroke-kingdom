The semicolon — never called that in Keystroke Kingdom. It is "the eighth key," "the final
key," "the key to the right of L," pressed by the right pinky. The player earns it at
Level 4 to complete the home row; its true name is withheld until after Level 20, when the
proposed "eighth-key confession" easter egg reveals the programmer's secret: it ends every
line of code.

## In this game

- Level 4's second new key, styled `?` in all UI — the Mystery Key that completes the home
  row and wakes the [[wopr-wargames|Mystery Cabinet]].
- The withholding is deliberate design (see CLAUDE.md and `.claude/rules/levels.md`):
  partly mystery-building, partly practical — the Lyria music tool cannot pronounce
  "semicolon."
- The planned reveal is proposed egg #9 in [[proposed-egg-references]]: *"THE EIGHTH KEY
  HAS A NAME. PROGRAMMERS END EVERY LINE WITH IT;"* — a rite of passage after the
  [[arpanet-first-message-1969|terminal level]], connecting the child's mystery key to
  [[view-source-culture|programmer culture]].
- [[ada-lovelace]]'s card lands at Level 4 — the first programmer arrives with the key
  programmers use most.
