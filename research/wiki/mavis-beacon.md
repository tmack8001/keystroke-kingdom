Mavis Beacon Teaches Typing is a touch-typing tutor released in late 1987 by The Software
Toolworks — one of the canonical, long-running commercial typing tutors that established
the home-row-first curriculum in home computing, predating Mario Teaches Typing by five
years.

## In this game

Mavis Beacon is cited in Keystroke Kingdom's research as one of the four canon tutors
(alongside [[mario-teaches-typing]], [[dance-mat-typing]], and [[typingclub]]) that all
share the same physiologically-grounded key ordering documented in
[[touch-typing-pedagogy]]. It's referenced as corroborating evidence that the
home-row-first, finger-pair-outward sequence is standard practice across the entire
touch-typing tutor genre, not a quirk of any single product — reinforcing the decision to
keep Keystroke Kingdom's own progression (documented in `.claude/rules/levels.md`, "Why
This Order") aligned with the canon rather than inventing a novel ordering.

## The facts

- Publisher: The Software Toolworks.
- **Release: late 1987** — predates [[mario-teaches-typing]] (1992) by five years, making
  it the earliest of the canon comparables gathered for this research.
- Curriculum: home-row-first touch typing, the same lineage documented across
  [[touch-typing-pedagogy]].
