Number Munchers (MECC, 1986) is the arcade-shaped math game of the American school computer
lab: a green Muncher moves around a grid eating the numbers that satisfy a rule printed at
the top of the screen — multiples, factors, primes, equalities, inequalities — while dodging
creatures called Troggles. It was designed by **R. Philip Bouchard**, who also designed the
[[oregon-trail|Oregon Trail]] series, and it is the clearest example in this wiki of the
era's dominant design: **a drill wearing a Pac-Man costume.**

## In this game

Number Munchers is included in [[educational-software]] deliberately as the *honest* case,
not the flattering one. It is a well-made, fondly remembered, genuinely effective program
that is nonetheless a timed evaluate-and-answer drill with enemies that can catch you — the
design [[educational-software]] argues the good exceptions ([[rockys-boots]],
[[oregon-trail]], [[gizmos-and-gadgets]]) were exceptions *from*.

For Keystroke Kingdom the relevant contrast is structural. Number Munchers is close kin to
our Level 3 Pac-Man cabinet in *appearance* — grid, muncher, pursuers — and its opposite in
*contract*: a Troggle catching you costs you something. Under the One Rule nothing in this
game may do that outside opt-in Survival, and even there wrong keys cost nothing. If a
Number Munchers-flavoured idea is ever built here, the grid and the rule-at-the-top are the
keepable parts; the pursuit penalty is not.

## The facts

- **Publisher and developer:** MECC. **Released for Apple II in 1986**, "then MS-DOS and Mac
  in 1990."
- **Designer:** R. Philip Bouchard conceived the game concept; he also designed The Oregon
  Trail series.
- **Gameplay:** "The player controls the muncher who must move around a grid and munch on
  numbers that match the logic rule above while avoiding the threatening troggles."
- **Five modes:** Multiples, Factors, Primes, Equalities and Inequalities.
- **Troggles:** five enemy types with distinct movement patterns — Reggies, Bashfuls,
  Helpers, Workers and Smarties.
- **Series:** a spin-off of *Word Munchers*; rebranded **Math Munchers** after
  [[the-learning-company|The Learning Company]]'s acquisition of [[mecc|MECC]].
- **Unverified this session:** sales figures, and whether being caught by a Troggle removed a
  life in all versions (the pursuit mechanic is documented; the exact penalty is not). The
  "drill with a penalty" characterisation above rests on the documented avoid-the-troggles
  mechanic — do not state a specific life-loss rule without checking.
