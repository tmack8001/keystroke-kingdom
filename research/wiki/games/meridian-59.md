Meridian 59 (Archetype Interactive, published by The 3DO Company; early access online
from December 15, 1995, commercial release October 7, 1996) is credited as the first
3D graphical MMORPG and one of the longest-running original online RPGs — notable for
a documented dual-layer social system of **guilds** (with sigils, guild halls, and
leadership voting) and **political factions** players allied with, distinct from and
more mechanically developed than anything found for [[island-of-kesmai]] or
[[neverwinter-nights-1991]] in this research pass.

## The facts

- **Development and release:** built by Archetype Interactive (founded by brothers
  Andrew and Chris Kirmse), published by The 3DO Company; ran in an early/beta form
  online from December 15, 1995, with commercial release October 7, 1996, under a
  flat-rate monthly subscription model.
- **First 3D graphical MMORPG:** distinguishes it technically from the earlier,
  2D/text-hybrid Kesmai and Neverwinter Nights lineages.
- **Guild system:** guilds had a dynamic voting system for changing leadership,
  customized sigils displayed on player shields, and guild halls that could be won or
  lost through gameplay — a more mechanically developed guild system than documented
  for any earlier candidate in this research pass.
- **Political factions, separate from guilds:** players could ally with the warring
  **Necromancer** and **Hunter** factions, each altering gameplay for members, with
  explicit rewards for killing members of the opposing faction. A later game era also
  documents players choosing among three political factions (Jonas D'Accor's rebels,
  Princess Kateriina's throne, Duke Akardius's rival throne in Tos), each offering
  bonuses to specific player types — this is the clearest single documented match in
  this entire research pass for the brief's literal phrase "clans/alliances/factions
  you JOINED."
- **Access method:** proprietary client software (later released open-source by the
  Kirmses); no telnet or terminal-based access documented in the sources checked this
  pass.
- **Longevity:** one of the longest-running original online RPGs, continuing (via
  open-source community servers) long past its commercial closure.
