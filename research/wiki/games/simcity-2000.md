SimCity 2000 (Maxis, December 1993), designed by Will Wright and co-developed with Fred
Haslam, introduced isometric city-building (replacing the original SimCity's top-down view),
underground infrastructure layers, and an expanded on-screen color palette.

## In this game

SimCity 2000 is the **Level 16 cabinet**, opening Act 4 (The Command Center) and teaching 5
and 6 — the first number keys, chosen because index fingers reach straight up from home
position, and "numbers as city, populations, budgets." See `.claude/rules/levels.md` Level
16 and `research/video-game-history.md`.

## The facts

- Designed by Will Wright, co-developed with Fred Haslam at Maxis. Macintosh version
  released December 1993; MS-DOS version February 1994. Maxis both developed and published
  (distinct from the original 1989 SimCity, which Brøderbund distributed).
- Introduced isometric view, underground infrastructure layers, and an expanded on-screen
  color palette.
