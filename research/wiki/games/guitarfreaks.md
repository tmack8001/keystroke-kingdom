*GuitarFreaks* (Konami's **Bemani** division, **16 February 1999**, arcade) put a
**guitar-shaped controller** on a rhythm cabinet — three coloured neck buttons (red, green,
blue; later yellow and pink were added) plus a **pick lever** to strum. Its drum sibling
*DrumMania* followed **10 July 1999**. Wikipedia states outright that GuitarFreaks "laid the
foundations for popular guitar-based rhythm games, such as the Guitar Hero series," and that
GuitarFreaks and DrumMania "inspired the development of popular North American console game
franchises Guitar Hero and Rock Band."

## In this game

The entry that corrects the lineage: **[[guitar-hero]] did not invent the plastic guitar** —
Konami did, six years earlier, in an arcade cabinet. Relevant to [[rhythm-games-by-era]] §3 and
§6 for one genuinely interesting reason:

- **The two-hand gesture.** GuitarFreaks asks the player to **hold a fret button with one hand
  while moving the pick lever with the other** — hold one thing, act with the other. That is
  structurally the same coordination Level 24 teaches with **Shift**. It is the only mechanic in
  the genre that rhymes with something already in the curriculum.
- **But it only reaches a keyboard by emulation** ([[frets-on-fire]] proved that works; F1–F5
  frets, Enter to pick). The keyboard would be pretending to be a guitar neck, not being itself.

**Punishment to strip (One Rule):** a **Groove Gauge** that depletes on misses, and per-note
grading **Perfect / Great / Good / OK / Miss** — the same object [[beatmania]] uses under the
same name. Both out.

**What to draw:** the real cabinet, with its guitar-shaped controller and pick lever.

## The facts

- **Developer:** Bemani (Konami's music division). **GuitarFreaks: 16 February 1999**;
  **DrumMania: 10 July 1999**.
- **Platforms:** arcade (System 573, later Bemani PC), PlayStation, PlayStation 2.
- **Controller:** electric-guitar shape with **three buttons — red, green, blue** — on the neck
  (yellow and pink added in later versions), a **pick lever** simulating strumming, and a metal
  knob for sound effects.
- **Play:** scrolling coloured bars in columns; hold the matching button and move the pick lever
  as the note reaches the fretting line. Judged Perfect / Great / Good / OK / Miss, with a
  depleting Groove Gauge.
- **Lineage:** explicitly credited as the foundation of the guitar-rhythm form and as an
  inspiration for both Guitar Hero and Rock Band.
