Zero Wing is a 1989 Japanese arcade shoot-'em-up; its mistranslated 1991 European Sega
Mega Drive port produced the opening cutscene line "All your base are belong to us,"
which became one of the internet's earliest viral memes starting around 2000–2001.

## In this game

Proposed but not yet built (see [[proposed-egg-references]] for the full menu):
inverted into kid-empowerment framing — "ALL YOUR KEYS ARE BELONG TO YOU." Gets its own
page (rather than folding into the proposed-eggs concept page) because it has enough
independently cited, dated material — arcade original vs. mistranslated port vs. meme
virality are three distinct, sourced dates — to stand alone.

## The facts

- **October 1989:** the *Zero Wing* arcade original releases in Japan.
- **July 1991:** the mistranslated English-language Sega Mega Drive port releases in
  Europe — this port, not the arcade original, is the actual source of the famous
  mistranslation. The July 1991 date is sourced to a contemporaneous *Computer and
  Video Games* magazine review (August 1991 issue), a stronger citation trail than the
  "1992" figure that circulates on some fan sites.
- **Corrected during the citation pass:** the original hub-doc list entry said "Zero
  Wing 1991" without disambiguating arcade-original vs. port. The two dates (1989
  arcade, 1991 port) are now spelled out explicitly so "1991" isn't ambiguous.
- The "All your base are belong to us" meme circulated in gaming-quote compilations by
  1998–99, then went fully viral via SomethingAwful (November 2000) and a Newgrounds
  Flash animation (February 16, 2001, credited to Bad-CRC) — both preserved copies
  exist (Internet Archive).
