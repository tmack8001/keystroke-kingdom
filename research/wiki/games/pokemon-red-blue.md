Pokémon Red and Green (Game Freak, February 27, 1996, Japan), designed and created by
Satoshi Tajiri with character/art direction by Ken Sugimori, took roughly six years to
develop and nearly bankrupted Game Freak before becoming a generation-defining franchise —
recombined and localized as Pokémon Red and Blue for North America in 1998.

## In this game

Pokémon Red/Blue is the **Level 15 cabinet**, teaching Z and slash and completing Act 3's
"FULL KEYBOARD UNLOCKED" milestone — every letter and core punctuation key lit
simultaneously. Pokémon names (Pikachu, Squirtle, Bulbasaur, Jigglypuff, Charizard) are used
as typing targets. See `.claude/rules/levels.md` Level 15.

## The facts

- Developed by Game Freak, published by Nintendo, designed/created by Satoshi Tajiri with
  character/art direction by Ken Sugimori. Development took roughly six years and nearly
  bankrupted Game Freak.
- Pokémon Red and Green released in Japan February 27, 1996; recombined and localized as
  Pokémon Red and Blue for North America in 1998.
