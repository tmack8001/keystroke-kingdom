*StepMania* (Chris Danford, **2001**, MIT licence) began as an open-source clone of
[[dance-dance-revolution]] and became the rhythm engine much of the field runs on. Its own site
describes it as *"a free dance and rhythm game for Windows, Mac, and Linux. It features 3D
graphics, **keyboard and 'dance pad' support**, and an editor for creating your own steps."*
Commercial arcade games built on it include *In the Groove*, *Pump It Up Pro* and *Pump It Up
Infinity*.

## In this game

Cited in [[rhythm-games-by-era]] §6 and [[rhythm-on-a-keyboard]] as the second proof that
**keyboard rhythm gaming is real, official and long-lived** — not a hack. StepMania has shipped
keyboard support as a first-class, documented feature for a quarter of a century, and commercial
arcade operators built cabinets on the same engine.

It also carries the same ceiling as [[frets-on-fire]]: **the arrow keys are pretending to be a
dance pad.** The keyboard is a controller, not the instrument, and the letters mean nothing.

And it contributes one practical point to [[rhythm-games-by-era]] §10: **it ships a step
editor**, because a rhythm game needs a **note chart** for every track, and charting is separate
labour from composing. Any music act here inherits that cost.

**Punishment to strip (One Rule):** it inherits DDR's — a life gauge and grading — though the
site notes "timing and scoring settings can easily be changed" from DDR's defaults, which is
itself a small precedent for a configurable-forgiveness design.

## The facts

- **Creator:** Chris Danford, **2001**, "an open-source clone of Konami's arcade game series
  Dance Dance Revolution." **Licence:** MIT (the site: "StepMania and its website are open
  source software released under the MIT License").
- **Input:** computer keyboard **or** dance pad. On a keyboard, "the keyboard can be used to tap
  out the rhythms using arrow or other keys." With a pad, arrows scroll upward to meet a
  stationary set of target arrows.
- **Features:** 3D graphics and a **step editor** for authoring charts.
- **Commercial engines built on it:** *In the Groove* (arcade), *Pump It Up Pro* (StepMania 4),
  *Pump It Up Infinity* (StepMania 5), and *StepManiaX* as In the Groove's spiritual successor.
- **Releases:** 5.0.12 stable with 5.1 in beta at the time of the site fetch.
