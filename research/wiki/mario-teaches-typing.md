Mario Teaches Typing (Interplay, 1992) is the closest direct comparable to Keystroke
Kingdom — a Nintendo-licensed touch-typing tutor that teaches the standard home-row-first,
finger-pair-outward curriculum under a single game skin. Released on floppy in 1992, with
an enhanced CD-ROM edition in 1993; a sequel, Mario Teaches Typing 2, followed from
Interplay (Brainstorm) in 1997.

## In this game

MTT is Trevor's own childhood reference point and the yardstick Keystroke Kingdom is built
against. It uses the identical proven key progression documented in
[[touch-typing-pedagogy]] — home row first, then reaches outward in finger-pairs (E/I →
R/U → T/Y → W/O → Q/P, then bottom row) — which is exactly the order [[touch-typing-pedagogy|Keystroke
Kingdom's own levels.md]] follows. The design departure is deliberate: MTT wears one Mario
skin for its entire runtime, where Keystroke Kingdom assigns one distinct arcade cabinet
per level with era-progressive graphics (see the Act structure in `.claude/rules/levels.md`)
so the visual world grows in fidelity alongside the player's skill. MTT also — like nearly
every canon tutor — lumps `` ` - = [ ] ' \ `` into a single "symbols" lesson or skips them
outright; Keystroke Kingdom's dedicated Corner Cabinet levels (21–23) are more thorough on
the outer keys than the canon it's descended from.

## The facts

- Developer/publisher: Interplay Entertainment Corp.
- **Release year: 1992** (floppy disk), with an enhanced CD-ROM edition following in 1993.
  This corrects an earlier internal note that had the year as 1991 — see the citation-pass
  correction below.
- Sequel: Mario Teaches Typing 2, Interplay, 1997 (some fan sites claim 1996; MobyGames and
  Wikipedia both give 1997).
- Curriculum: same lineage as [[mavis-beacon]], [[dance-mat-typing]], and [[typingclub]] —
  home row first, then finger-pair reaches outward. The ordering is physiological (which
  finger reaches which key), not a stylistic choice specific to any one product. See
  [[touch-typing-pedagogy]] for the full comparative history.

**Correction from citation pass (2026-07-11):** an earlier draft of this research dated
MTT to 1991. The correct release year is **1992** (floppy), confirmed independently by
Wikipedia, MobyGames, and an Internet Archive listing. Publisher (Interplay) was already
correct.
