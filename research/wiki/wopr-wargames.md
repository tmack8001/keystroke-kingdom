WOPR ("War Operation Plan Response") is the NORAD supercomputer in the 1983 film
*WarGames*, famous for the line "Shall we play a game?" and "Greetings, Professor
Falken" — the film that taught a generation that terminals talk back, and that also
prompted the first-ever US presidential directive on computer security (NSDD-145).

## In this game

WOPR is the original secret of Keystroke Kingdom's easter-egg system: typing
`wopr`/`wargames`/`shallweplayagame` on the title screen unlocks a level-select screen
styled as the WOPR terminal, greeting the player with "GREETINGS PROFESSOR FALKEN." See
[[hacker-culture]] and [[easter-egg-culture]] for the design philosophy behind the
system this egg founded.

## The facts

- **June 3, 1983:** *WarGames* released.
- WOPR and the AI character "Joshua" both speak the film's two most quoted lines:
  "Shall we play a game?" and "Greetings, Professor Falken."
- The film's depiction of a computer nearly triggering nuclear war reportedly prompted
  a Camp David screening for President Reagan and contributed to NSDD-145, the first
  presidential directive on computer security.
