"Riddles in the Dark" is the fifth chapter of J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Hobbit* (first
published September 21, 1937, George Allen & Unwin) — the chapter where Bilbo Baggins
and Gollum play a life-or-death riddle-game in a cave, and where Bilbo finds the One
Ring. Several of the riddles exchanged are not Tolkien's own invention but drawn from
older folk-riddle traditions, which he — a professional philologist — would have known
intimately.

## The facts

- **September 21, 1937:** *The Hobbit, or There and Back Again* published by George
  Allen & Unwin, initial print run 1,500 copies, sold out by December on the strength
  of enthusiastic reviews.
- The riddle-game is a wager: if Gollum stumps Bilbo, Bilbo is eaten; if Bilbo stumps
  Gollum, Gollum must show him the way out. Bilbo ultimately wins with a non-riddle
  ("What have I got in my pocket?") after running out of real riddles — a plot detail
  Tolkien revised for the 1951 second edition to fit *The Lord of the Rings*'
  continuity (the 1937 first edition has Gollum losing his ring to Bilbo amicably as a
  wager stake; the revision makes Gollum's loss involuntary and vindictive).
- **Not all original to Tolkien.** Multiple sources note the riddles draw on two
  different traditions in-story: "Gollum's riddles are taken from old historical
  sources, while those of Bilbo come from modern nursery books" — i.e. even within the
  chapter, Tolkien is consciously working with pre-existing riddle material, not
  inventing all of it from scratch.
- **The six riddles used in this game's Act 6 bank**, with confirmed exact text:
  - **TIME:** "This thing all things devours: Birds, beasts, trees, flowers; Gnaws
    iron, bites steel; Grinds hard stones to meal; Slays king, ruins town, And beats
    high mountain down." — Gollum's riddle. Answer: time.
  - **TEETH:** "Thirty white horses on a red hill, First they champ, Then they stamp,
    Then they stand still." — Bilbo's riddle. Answer: teeth. (Often noted as
    technically imprecise — a full adult set is 32 teeth, not 30 — but this is the
    riddle's traditional wording, not a Tolkien error to correct.)
  - **EGG:** "A box without hinges, key, or lid, Yet golden treasure inside is hid." —
    Bilbo's riddle. Answer: egg.
  - **FISH:** "Alive without breath, As cold as death; Never thirsty, ever drinking,
    All in mail never clinking." — Gollum's riddle. Answer: fish.
  - **DARK:** "It cannot be seen, cannot be felt, Cannot be heard, cannot be smelt, It
    lies behind stars and under hills, And empty holes it fills, It comes first and
    follows after, Ends life, kills laughter." — Gollum's riddle. Answer: the dark.
  - **WIND:** "Voiceless it cries, Wingless flutters, Toothless bites, Mouthless
    mutters." — Gollum's riddle. Answer: wind.
- **Copyright note (design-relevant, not a historical claim):** the text of *The
  Hobbit* remains under copyright to the Tolkien Estate/HarperCollins. The exact
  wordings above are quoted here for identification/citation purposes only, per
  standard research practice. See `research/act6-riddle-bank.md`'s downstream note —
  any of these riddles that ship as actual in-game text should be original,
  warmly-reworded paraphrases inspired by the imagery and wordplay, not verbatim lines.
