XYZZY is the invented magic word from Will Crowther's original Colossal Cave Adventure
(c. 1975–76), expanded by Don Woods in 1977 — typing it produces the canonical response
"Nothing happens," and it went on to become a recurring easter-egg convention across
decades of later software.

## In this game

Title screen: typing `xyzzy` triggers `NOTHING HAPPENS.` — the joke IS the accuracy,
since that's the actual canonical in-game response from [[colossal-cave-adventure]]
itself. See [[hacker-culture]] and [[easter-egg-culture]].

## The facts

- Will Crowther writes the original Adventure c. 1975–76 (sources vary between "1975,"
  "1975–76," and "c. 1976" — this page uses "1976–77" to reflect Crowther's original
  work through Don Woods' 1977 expansion, the most common framing).
- XYZZY is Crowther's invented word; the canonical "Nothing happens" response is
  confirmed.
- XYZZY's influence spread as an easter-egg convention into later software, including
  Data General AOS and Windows Minesweeper.
