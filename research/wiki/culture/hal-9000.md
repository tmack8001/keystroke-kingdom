HAL 9000 is the onboard computer antagonist of Stanley Kubrick's *2001: A Space Odyssey*
(1968) — the most famous fictional computer in film, remembered for playing chess,
reading lips, and turning on its crew.

## In this game

Level 5's history card spells `hal`. G and H just unlocked at this level, so the most
famous fictional computer arrives right as the reach keys do. Card text: *"The most
famous movie computer ever. HAL could talk, play chess, and read lips."* See
[[computing-history-moments]] for the full 17-card table.

## The facts

- **1968:** *2001: A Space Odyssey* releases, directed by Stanley Kubrick, co-written
  with Arthur C. Clarke.
- HAL plays an in-film chess game against astronaut Frank Poole; the game is a real
  historical match (Roesch–Schlage, Hamburg 1910) re-enacted for the film.
- HAL's lip-reading plot point (HAL watches the astronauts' lips through a window to
  learn they plan to disconnect it) is a confirmed plot element.
- Verdict: accurate, no correction needed — year and both capability claims confirmed.
