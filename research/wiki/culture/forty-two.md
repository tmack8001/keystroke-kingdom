42 is Deep Thought's Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and
Everything, from Douglas Adams' *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* — first
established in the BBC Radio 4 series (first broadcast March 8, 1978), then carried
into the 1979 novel. Adams himself said the choice was simply a joke: "It was a
joke... I chose that one."

## The facts

- **March 8, 1978:** first BBC Radio 4 broadcast of *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the
  Galaxy*; the "42"/Deep Thought/Ultimate Question material originates here.
- **October 12, 1979:** novel publication (Pan Books, UK), adapted from the radio
  series.
- Douglas Adams explicitly said 42 was an arbitrary joke choice, not a hidden numeric
  meaning — directly quoted in Scientific American.
- The hub doc's dual-date framing ("1978 (radio)/1979 (novel)") replaced an earlier
  version that cited only "1979" — corrected during the citation pass to credit the
  earlier radio origin.
