Masters of Deception (MOD) was a New York-based hacker/phreak group of the late
1980s and early 1990s, best known as the rival of the [[legion-of-doom|Legion of
Doom]] (LOD) in what's remembered as the "Great Hacker War." MOD focused on exploring
Regional Bell Operating Company phone switches and the minicomputers/mainframes that
administered them. Five of its members were indicted by a joint FBI/Secret Service
task force in 1992 and all pleaded guilty in 1993.

## The facts

- **Formed in the late 1980s** in New York City, as a rival crew to the Legion of
  Doom.
- **Name is a deliberate mockery of LOD:** "M" sits one letter past "L" in the
  alphabet — read by the underground as a statement that LOD had lost its edge.
- **Focus:** exploiting Regional Bell Operating Company phone switches and the
  minicomputers and mainframes used to administer the telephone network.
- As federal prosecutions weakened LOD in the late 1980s, some hackers — notably
  Phiber Optik — joined MOD, described as an effort to "restore the direction of the
  spirit of underground hacking."
- **1992 indictment, 1993 guilty pleas:** a joint FBI/Secret Service task force
  indicted five MOD members in federal court in 1992; within six months all five had
  pleaded guilty and were sentenced to either probation or prison, prosecuted by the
  US Attorney's office for the Southern District of New York.
