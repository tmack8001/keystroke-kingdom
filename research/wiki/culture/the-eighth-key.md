The semicolon — not called that in Keystroke Kingdom until Level 13 teaches its name (Trevor, 2026-10-31). Until then it is "the eighth key," "the final
key," "the key to the right of L," pressed by the right pinky. The player earns it at
Level 4 to complete the home row; its name is taught at Level 13 (the planned teaching beat), and the
"eighth-key confession" easter egg at Level 20 reveals the programmer's secret: many languages end an
instruction with it. *(Corrected 2026-10-31: this page and the egg said "every line of code" —
false; see [[semicolon]] for the verified name, origin and code claims.)*

## In this game

- Level 4's second new key, styled `;` in all UI (it stood in as `?` until 2026-10-30) — the Mystery Key that completes the home
  row and wakes the [[wopr-wargames|Mystery Cabinet]].
- The withholding is deliberate design (see CLAUDE.md and `.claude/rules/levels.md`):
  partly mystery-building, partly practical — the Lyria music tool cannot pronounce
  "semicolon."
- The planned reveal is proposed egg #9 in [[proposed-egg-references]]: `THE EIGHTH KEY
  HAS A NAME.` / `MANY CODERS END AN INSTRUCTION WITH IT;` *(the original wording, "PROGRAMMERS END EVERY LINE WITH IT;", was false and was corrected 2026-10-31)* — a rite of passage after the
  [[arpanet-first-message-1969|terminal level]], connecting the child's mystery key to
  [[view-source-culture|programmer culture]].
- [[ada-lovelace]]'s card lands at Level 4 — the first programmer arrives with the key
  programmers use most.
