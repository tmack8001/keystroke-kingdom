TypingClub is a contemporary, web-based touch-typing tutor used widely in K-12 schools,
carrying forward the same home-row-first curriculum as the older canon tutors into the
modern browser-based era.

## In this game

TypingClub is the most modern of the four canon comparables gathered for this research
(alongside [[mario-teaches-typing]], [[mavis-beacon]], and [[dance-mat-typing]]), and its
own teacher-facing handbook is cited directly as a curriculum source for
[[touch-typing-pedagogy]] — confirming that even present-day, institutionally-deployed
tutors keep the identical home-row-first, finger-to-key sequence Keystroke Kingdom follows.
As a browser-first, zero-install product aimed at classrooms, TypingClub is also the
closest sibling to Keystroke Kingdom's own "browser-first, zero install friction" platform
philosophy documented in `.claude/rules/tech.md`.

## The facts

- Format: web-based touch-typing software, used at grades K-12.
- Curriculum: home-row-first, finger-to-key assignment, progressive outward sequencing —
  documented in TypingClub's own teacher handbook ("Typing Jungle Teacher Handbook").
- **Sourcing note:** do not cite Common Sense Education for TypingClub — its edtech review
  program was discontinued in early 2026.
